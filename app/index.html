How to interpret this assessment

  • Energy is a unitless pressure index, not electricity price or MW.
  • Carbon uses tonnes of CO₂ equivalent, but the recipe does not define an annual or lifecycle accounting period.
  • Species records are incomplete. Available source data does not imply complete coverage at this location.
  • Country assignment and spatial screening are approximate; the current geometry calculations ignore polygon holes. The model does not simulate river flow, pollution dispersion, or species population changes.
  • Habitat, vegetation, and river labels do not model a time trajectory. The selected year primarily changes the synthetic jobs and carbon assumptions.
