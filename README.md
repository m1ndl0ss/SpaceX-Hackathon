Yosemite is a prototype platform connecting community environmental action with government planning. Activists can report environmental violations and join calls for help, while government teams can review reports, coordinate volunteers, and track responses through a unified dashboard. An integrated simulation tool uses pretrained models to explore illustrative environmental impacts of proposed infrastructure projects and compare mitigation options. Yosemite brings reporting, coordination, and impact exploration into one accessible interface to support more informed conservation decisions.
